# Services

Cadre's four core services are AI Strategy, AI Leadership & Facilitation, AI Engineering and AI Agents. It also offers AI Data & Dashboards.

## AI Strategy
<!-- src: https://cadre.ai/strategy -->
- For mid-market and enterprise leadership teams. It usually starts with the **AI Transformation Intensive**.
- Four steps:
  1. **Discover use cases.** Interview departments, estimate ROI, and rank use cases by impact and feasibility.
  2. **Survey the landscape.** Review existing tools, the AI capabilities expected in the next 3–6 months, and the gaps.
  3. **Implement solutions.** Deploy, train staff, and refine.
  4. **Scale with confidence.** Extend to other departments, appoint internal champions, and build a 3-year roadmap.
- Built on Cadre's **8 Pillars of AI Transformation**:
  - AI team
  - AI command center
  - cultural change
  - tech stack integration
  - data readiness
  - agent readiness
  - departmental deep dives
  - a 3-year AI vision
- Cadre helps implement. It doesn't leave behind a slide deck.

## AI Transformation Intensive
<!-- src: https://cadre.ai/ai-transformation-intensive -->
- A structured 45-day program, "from kickoff to 12-month roadmap within 45 days."
- Phases: Kickoff → AI Maturity Index → Full-Day Workshop → Use Case Library → Three-Year Vision → Twelve-Month Roadmap. Each phase produces a deliverable.

## AI Leadership & Facilitation
<!-- src: https://cadre.ai/leadership-facilitation -->
- Executive facilitation: "Not training. Not lectures."
- Formats:
  - 1-Hour Virtual Kickoff
  - Half-Day Executive Session
  - 1-Day Workshop
  - 2-Day Leadership Intensive
- Time split: about 30% teaching, 30% interaction (including role-played resistance scenarios) and 40% applying it to the client's own departments.
- Leaders leave with 3–5 specific problems, a prioritized shortlist (quick wins vs. long-term plays) and an action plan.

## AI Engineering
<!-- src: https://cadre.ai/ai-engineering -->
- Automates manual work such as data entry, document routing, email triage and report generation.
- Three steps:
  1. Understand the problem.
  2. Pick the right approach: existing tools, workflow automation, or custom agents.
  3. Integrate and automate.
- Cadre is pragmatic: sometimes an inexpensive existing tool plus smart automation beats custom development.

## AI Agents
<!-- src: https://cadre.ai/agents -->
- Three tiers:
  - **Prompts & Assistants.**
  - **Voice Agents** for call intake, qualification, support and routing.
  - **Fully fledged AI agents** that run with guardrails and human oversight.
- Example agents from Cadre's library. Describe these as agents Cadre offers ("Cadre's agent library includes an Automated Underwriter, built to…"), not as work delivered for a particular client:
  - Automated Underwriter ("underwrite loans in minutes, not days")
  - Automated Takeoff Generator (construction)
  - Access Request Provisioner
  - Ad Copy Tester
  - Audit & Compliance Tracker

## AI Data & Dashboards
<!-- src: https://cadre.ai/data-and-dashboards -->
- Connects systems such as CRM, ERP, accounting, HR and payroll into one data warehouse, with a semantic layer and data models for 3–5 use cases. It's typically a 60-day build.
- Unstructured documents (contracts, SOPs, emails) are scoped and priced separately by a solution architect.
