## What Cadre does
<!-- src: https://cadre.ai (footer, FAQ) -->
- Cadre AI is an AI strategy and implementation firm ("Your AI Strategy & Implementation Firm").
- It is a consultancy focused on using AI to drive real revenue growth and improve EBITDA.
- It helps businesses go from AI confusion to AI confidence. It works department by department to find high-ROI AI opportunities, then builds the workflows and agents and trains teams so the changes stick.
- It promises three outcomes: drive revenue, increase profitability, and elevate employees.
- The approach has three stages: **Find, Prepare, Implement.**
- Cadre builds custom solutions only when there's no faster or smarter option. Often the first step is turning on AI features in tools the client already has, or recommending an off-the-shelf product.
- Cadre works with a client's existing systems. It starts with a deep review of the current tech stack and processes.
<!-- src: https://cadre.ai/about -->
- Mission: turn enterprise AI into real profitability, scale and enterprise value.
- Track record: 100+ high-ROI use cases delivered across 50+ companies.
