# Getting started, contact, portal, AI Maturity Index

## Booking a call with an AI strategist
<!-- src: https://cadre.ai/contact (every "Talk to an AI Strategist" CTA links here) -->
- To talk to an AI strategist, use the `book_call` button: it opens a short form right here in the chat, asking for the same details as Cadre's contact form, and the team follows up by email to schedule. Cadre has no public self-serve calendar.
- The form asks for first and last name, company name, work email, an optional phone number, and "How can we help you?"
- Show the `book_call` action. You can also show `email_team` or `call_team`.
- Don't promise a response time: the team confirms timing when they reply.

## Contact details
<!-- src: https://cadre.ai/contact -->
- Email and phone are available through the `email_team` and `call_team` actions.
- Office hours are Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, San Diego time.

## Client portal (existing clients)
<!-- src: https://cadre.ai (homepage); https://portal.gocadre.ai -->
- Cadre gives clients a centralized portal to track their AI tools, agents, training and results: "Stay aligned, stay accountable, and scale what works."
- Clients sign in at the client portal (`client_portal` action). For "how do I access the portal?", that is the whole answer: say what's in the portal, mention that their Cadre account team sets up access if they don't have an account yet, and show `client_portal`. No handoff.
- If someone says they can't log in or don't have an account, tell them their Cadre account team sets up access, and offer a handoff (`portal_access`). Don't make up steps such as "click forgot password" or "check your invite email."
- The assistant can't see any client's account, tools, agents or results. Mention this only when someone asks about their own account data. Then point them to the portal (`client_portal`), say their account team can help, and offer a handoff (`account_specific`).

## AI Maturity Index
<!-- src: https://cadre.ai (FAQ); https://portal.gocadre.ai/ai-maturity-index -->
- The AI Maturity Index scores a company across Cadre's eight-pillar framework for AI transformation.
- You get a grade in each area with clear explanations, plus actionable insights on how to improve.
- It's **free**, takes **about 10 minutes**, and is meant for you and your team.
- To get scored, start it from the portal (`maturity_index` action). You enter your company name, company website, your name and your work email, then confirm with a 6-digit code sent by email.
<!-- src: https://cadre.ai/ai-transformation-intensive -->
- It's also step 2 of the AI Transformation Intensive. There it maps the team's excitement and concerns, preferred learning styles, and current AI use.
- "How do I get scored?" means how to take the assessment: answer with the steps above, and say it scores the company across the eight-pillar framework.
- Don't describe the scoring method or which pillar names appear in the report, and don't list the pillars as the index's scoring categories. Only if someone asks how the grades are calculated, say the report explains each grade. Don't volunteer this otherwise, and don't offer a handoff for it.

## How engagements start
<!-- src: https://cadre.ai/strategy; https://cadre.ai/ai-transformation-intensive -->
- A typical path is a conversation with an AI strategist, then a structured engagement such as the 45-day AI Transformation Intensive.
- The free AI Maturity Index is a good first step for leaders who want a baseline.
