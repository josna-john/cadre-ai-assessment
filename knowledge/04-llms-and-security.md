# LLM selection and data security

## Partners and model choice

- Cadre is an **Anthropic and OpenAI partner**. It works with technology partners including Microsoft, AWS, Salesforce, Snowflake and Meta.
<!-- src: https://cadre.ai/articles/cadre-ai-selected-as-an-official-openai-service-partner -->
- Cadre was selected as an official OpenAI Service Partner (announced January 2026). That work covers ChatGPT for Business adoption, custom GPTs, agents and training.
<!-- src: https://cadre.ai/ai-engineering; https://cadre.ai (model logos) -->
- Cadre is model-agnostic: it chooses the right LLM for each business and use case. It works across Claude, GPT/OpenAI, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Mistral, Meta's models and others, including model routing through OpenRouter.
<!-- src: https://cadre.ai/articles/ai-model-selection -->
- How Cadre thinks about model tiers, from its published article:
  - Smaller, faster models (such as Claude Haiku) suit high-volume classification and extraction.
  - Mid-tier models (such as Claude Sonnet) are the practical default for most AI-enabled workflows.
  - Top-tier models (such as Claude Opus) should be a deliberate choice with a stated reason.

## Data security
<!-- src: https://cadre.ai/ai-engineering -->
- Cadre's data commitments:
  - "black-box" client data so it's never used to train other models
  - stop employees from sharing company secrets with personal LLM accounts
  - get the whole team onto secure, compliant AI tools
<!-- src: https://cadre.ai/data-and-dashboards -->
- For the AI Data & Dashboards offering specifically:
  - each customer gets an isolated instance on a SOC 2 Type II platform
  - zero AI data retention, under signed agreements with the AI providers
  - client data never trains a model
  - row-level security controls who sees what at query time
- **Be precise:** the SOC 2 Type II statement describes the platform used for Data & Dashboards. It isn't a statement that Cadre itself holds a SOC 2 certification.
